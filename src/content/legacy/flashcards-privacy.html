Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Momentum Apps Limited ("we," "our," or "us"), based in New Zealand, operates the StudyMap - Flashcards mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains how we handle your data as you use our study tools and purchase premium content.

The Core Privacy Principle: Your flashcards belong to you. We do not host your personal decks on our servers, and we do not have an account system — there is no sign-up or sign-in. Most of your data stays exactly where it belongs: on your device. The App contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising or ad SDKs.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the limited technical information needed to verify paid deck purchases, deliver the purchased deck files, and protect our download service from abuse. We do not collect your name, email address (unless you email us), contacts, location, or the content of your flashcards.

1.1 Purchase Information

When you make a one-time purchase in our Shop (the paid HSK 2.0 and TOPIK decks), the transaction is processed by the Google Play Store using Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment; we never see or store your credit card or other payment details. The App keeps a copy of your Google Play purchase receipt on your device.

When you download a paid deck, the App sends the following to our purchase-verification service (a Google Firebase Cloud Function) so it can confirm the purchase is genuine:

  • The Product ID (e.g., "hsk_level_1_en").
  • The Google Play purchase receipt, which includes the Order ID, purchase token, the App's package name, the Product ID, the purchase time, and the purchase state.
  • Google Play's digital signature for that receipt.

The service checks the signature and, if the purchase is valid, returns temporary download links (valid for 10 minutes) for the deck files. The service's logs record the Product ID, the number of files, and the link expiry time — not the raw receipt. The App confirms (acknowledges) the purchase with Google Play only after the deck has been imported successfully. If a purchase is never delivered, Google Play automatically refunds an unacknowledged purchase after about 3 days.

1.2 App Integrity and Technical Identifiers

To make sure only the genuine StudyMap app can request purchased deck files, we use Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity. When you download a paid deck, the App sends an integrity token generated by Google Play so our service can confirm the request comes from an unmodified copy of the App installed from Google Play.

The Firebase software used in the App also uses a Firebase Installation ID — a random identifier for this installation of the App that does not identify you or your physical device. As with any internet request, Google's servers also receive your device's IP address when the App contacts our purchase-verification service or downloads deck files. Your IP address may appear in Google Cloud request logs for the purchase-verification service and deck file storage; these logs are kept for 30 days, like our other logs.

Google's Cloud Functions SDK may also include a Firebase Cloud Messaging token with requests to our purchase-verification service, even though StudyMap does not send notifications.

We use this information only to deliver purchased content and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not use it for analytics, advertising, or tracking, and we do not sell it.

We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising. The App does not include Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, or any other analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK, and it does not record in-app events, usage statistics, or crash reports.

2. Information Stored Locally

The following data is stored locally on your device (in the App's private database and settings) and is never sent to Momentum Apps Limited or Firebase:

  • Flashcard content you create or import (CSV/Excel/JSON). Decks you create or import are never uploaded.
  • Your specific study progress, SRS intervals, difficulty ratings, review history, and statistics.
  • Your local deck organization (groups, pinned decks) and settings (including daily study limits, theme, and language).
  • Your Google Play purchase receipts, used to remember which decks you own.

Importing a file uses the Android system file picker, so the App only reads the specific file you choose. The App does not request storage permissions; its only declared permission is internet access (Google's libraries may add the permissions needed for Google Play Billing and checking network status). The free alphabet decks (Hiragana, Katakana, and Hangeul) are included in the App and do not need to be downloaded. If you turn on audio, pronunciation playback uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Android backup: The App allows Android's standard Auto Backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the App's local database and settings to your own Google account, and restore them when you reinstall the App or set up a new device. This backup is managed by Android and Google under your Google account; the data is not sent to Momentum Apps Limited and we cannot access it. You can turn off backup in your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services

To process payments, verify purchases, and deliver purchased decks, we use the following third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy practices:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing: Handles payments, refunds, purchase records, and app distribution. Google Play Integrity checks that the App is genuine. Google Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase (Google): Used for purchase verification (Cloud Functions), app integrity checks (App Check), and hosting the paid deck files (Cloud Storage). Firebase is not used for analytics or crash reporting. Privacy in Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Purchase verification and deck file delivery are processed on Google's servers in the United States (our purchase-verification service runs in Google Cloud's us-central1 region), so this data is processed outside New Zealand.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not use an account system, we cannot "delete your account" upon request as no such account exists.

To delete your local data in the App: Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete my data ("Erase decks, progress, and settings on this device"):

  1. A dialog titled "Delete everything on this device?" lists what will be deleted:

    • all decks and cards;
    • review history and SRS progress;
    • statistics and streaks;
    • settings and preferences, including daily study limits;
    • imported and downloaded decks stored on the device;
    • local records of which decks were imported.

    It also lists what is not affected:

    • Your Google Play purchases stay on your Google account and can be restored or redownloaded from the Shop.
    • An Android Auto Backup copy is managed by Google. You can remove it via your phone's Settings > Google > Backup or at https://one.google.com/backup.

    Tap Cancel to stop, or Continue.

  2. The next screen warns "This cannot be undone." Type DELETE and tap the red "Delete my data" button. Cancel is still available at this step.

  3. The App shows "Deleting your data…" and then "Your data has been deleted." Tap "Start fresh" to restart the App.

You can also delete your local data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.

To request deletion of data we hold: You can instead email us at support@momentum-apps.com to ask us to delete any data Momentum Apps Limited holds about you, such as your support emails and purchase-verification function logs.

Purchase verification data: The receipt and signature sent to our verification service are used to check your purchase and are not stored by us; the service's logs keep only the Product ID, file count, and link expiry time. Google Cloud request logs for the service and for deck file storage may also include your IP address. All of these logs are kept in Google Cloud Logging for 30 days and then deleted. Temporary download links expire after 10 minutes.

Google Play records: Your purchase history and order records are held by Google under Google's own policies. You can view them in your Google Play order history.

5. Children's Privacy

StudyMap is a general audience study tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with information via a purchase or support email, please contact us.

6. New Zealand Privacy Act & GDPR

As a New Zealand-based company, we comply with the Privacy Act 2020. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process purchase verification data because it is necessary to fulfil our contract with you (delivering the decks you purchased), and we process app integrity information based on our legitimate interest in protecting our service and your purchases from fraud and abuse. We do not process any data for analytics or advertising.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you. You may contact us at support@momentum-apps.com to ask about, access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. You can delete the data stored on your device yourself at any time (see Section 4). We keep support emails only as long as needed to resolve your request, and no longer than 12 months.
