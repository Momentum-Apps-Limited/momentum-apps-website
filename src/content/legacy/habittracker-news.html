[v1.2.0] - 2025-03-05

New Feature: Grace Days

Introducing Grace Days! Now you can protect your streak even if you miss a scheduled habit.

How it Works:

  • A Grace Day prevents your streak from breaking but does not count as a completion.
  • Grace Days do not increment your streak—only actual completions do.
  • Each habit frequency has different Grace Day limits
  • You cannot use consecutive Grace Days—this enforces the Never Miss Twice Rule to help you stay on track!
